About the Project

What It Does

Deforestation Watch processes multi-decadal satellite imagery to map, quantify, and visualize changes in Nepal's forest canopy cover over time.

Why It Matters

Forests prevent landslides, safeguard biodiversity, and sustain local communities in Nepal. Timely monitoring helps detect illegal logging and ecological degradation early.

Project Goal

To provide researchers, conservationists, and the public with clear, visual geospatial analysis to support data-informed decision-making in Nepal.

Key Project Insights

  • Increasing Trend: Forest canopy loss in Nepal has shown an overall upward trajectory over the 22-year observation period.
  • Event Spikes: Specific years (such as 2017) recorded significantly higher loss due to combined anthropogenic pressures and climate anomalies.
  • Remote Sensing Efficacy: Satellite monitoring provides an efficient, reproducible method to identify priority zones for reforestation.
